about

TypeIt is a typing test with words, phrases and code, live races against other players, a daily challenge, lessons and drills built from your own mistakes, and an account that keeps your history so you can see yourself improve.

word set

Words tests use the most common words of the chosen language. Phrases use real passages (classics, quotes, speeches), and code tests use short snippets in several languages.

keybinds

Esc gives you new text, Tab restarts the same text and M toggles the sound. In zen mode, Shift + Enter ends the run. Right-click (or the ⋯ button) on players, notifications and admin rows opens a menu of actions.

stats

wpm
correctly typed characters (including spaces) divided by 5, normalised to 60 seconds.
raw wpm
calculated like wpm, but counting every typed character, mistakes included.
acc
percentage of keys pressed correctly.
char
correct characters / mistakes / backspaces, worked out after the test.
consistency
how steady your speed was, from the variation of your raw speed second by second. Closer to 100% is better.

results screen

After a test you see your speed, raw speed, accuracy, character counts and how your speed moved over the run, plus your slowest words. Verified runs count for rankings and badges; runs that fail the integrity checks are saved but never ranked.
